# Merchandising Guide
SAMPLE DOCUMENT - Created for this demo only. It is not a real retailer's guide.

## Planograms
Follow the current planogram for each department. Do not move a product to a different shelf without approval from the merchandising lead. Check planogram compliance every Monday and fix any errors the same day.

## Price Changes
Price changes take effect on Monday mornings. Update shelf edge labels before the store opens on Monday. If a label is missing or wrong, remove the product from sale until the label is corrected.

## Endcaps and Promotions
Endcap displays change every two weeks. A promotion sign must show the promotion price, the start date, and the end date. Remove expired promotion signs on the day the promotion ends.

## Seasonal Resets
Seasonal resets happen four times a year, in January, April, July, and October. Complete the reset checklist and photograph the finished display before signing it off.
