# Returns Policy
SAMPLE DOCUMENT - Created for this demo only. It is not a real retailer's policy.

## Standard Returns
Customers may return an item within 30 days of purchase with a receipt for a full refund to the original payment method. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

## Returns Without a Receipt
Without a receipt, a return is limited to store credit at the lowest selling price in the last 30 days. Photo identification is required for a return without a receipt over 50 dollars.

## Defective Items
Defective items may be exchanged within 90 days of purchase, with or without a receipt, if the defect is confirmed by a team member. A refund for a defective item requires manager approval.

## Final Sale Items
Clearance items marked final sale cannot be returned or exchanged. The final sale notice must be shown on the shelf edge label and on the receipt.
