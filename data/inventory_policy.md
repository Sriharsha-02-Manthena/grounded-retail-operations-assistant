# Inventory Policy
SAMPLE DOCUMENT - Created for this demo only. It is not a real retailer's policy.

## Reorder Thresholds
Reorder an item when its on-hand quantity falls to its reorder point. The reorder point is average daily sales multiplied by lead time in days, plus safety stock. High velocity items carry 7 days of safety stock. Low velocity items carry 14 days of safety stock.

## Cycle Counts
Count high velocity items every week. Count medium velocity items every two weeks. Count low velocity items once a month. Record any variance over 2 percent and investigate it before the next count.

## Receiving Stock
Check every delivery against its purchase order within 24 hours of arrival. Record damaged or short items on the delivery note and notify the supplier within 2 business days. Do not put damaged stock on the sales floor.

## Stockouts
If an item is out of stock, check nearby stores first, then check the warehouse. If no stock is available, tell the customer the expected restock date from the supplier update and offer a comparable substitute only if the customer asks for one.
